For My Baby Julie

♡

My dearest Julie,

I don't really know where to begin because there are so many things I want to tell you, but I guess I'll start with the simplest one: I love you.

I love you in the quiet moments, in the random conversations, in the little jokes, in the moments when we don't even know what to say, and even in the days when everything feels a little heavy. I love getting to know you little by little, and I love discovering the little things that make you the person you are.

Sometimes I catch myself smiling just because I remembered something you said. Sometimes I look at my phone hoping to see your name. And sometimes, without even realizing it, you become the person I want to tell things to first.

You have this way of making ordinary moments feel special. A simple conversation with you can make my day better. A small message from you can change my mood. And seeing you smile is honestly one of those things I could never get tired of.

“If I could choose one person to keep beside me through all the ordinary days, I would still choose you.”

I want you to know that you don't have to be perfect for me. You don't have to always be happy. You don't have to pretend that everything is okay when it isn't. I want to know the real you — your happy days, your tired days, your quiet days, your silly moments, your worries, your dreams, and everything in between.

If someday you feel tired, I hope you remember that there is someone here who genuinely cares about you. Someone who wants to see you succeed. Someone who wants to see you healthy, happy, peaceful, and proud of yourself.

I may not always know the perfect words to say. I may sometimes be awkward. I may not always know exactly what you need. But one thing I never want you to question is how much I care about you.

I will always love you, Julie. ❤️

Not only when everything is beautiful. Not only when we're laughing. Not only when things are easy. I want my love to be something that stays even when life gets complicated.

I want to be someone who encourages you when you doubt yourself, someone who celebrates you when you succeed, someone who listens when you need to talk, and someone who can simply sit beside you when words aren't necessary.

I don't know exactly what the future will look like. I don't know all the things that life will bring us. But if I am given the chance, I would love to continue discovering those days with you.

I want more conversations. More laughs. More random pictures. More late-night talks. More dates. More memories. More moments where we look at each other and realize how lucky we are to have crossed paths.

And if there are days when you feel like you're not enough, please remember this:

You are more than enough.
You are worth loving.
You are worth choosing.
You are worth protecting.
You are worth appreciating.
And you are incredibly special to me. ❤️

Thank you for allowing me to know you. Thank you for the smiles. Thank you for the conversations. Thank you for the little moments that probably seem simple, but mean much more to me than you realize.

I don't want this page to be just another sweet message. I want it to be a little reminder that somewhere in this world, there is someone who thinks about you, cares about you, wishes good things for you, and loves you deeply.

So whenever you're having a bad day, come back here. Read this again. And remember that you are loved.

My love for you isn't something I want to say only once. I want to show it through the little things. Through patience. Through effort. Through understanding. Through being there. Through choosing you again and again.

And no matter how many times I say it, I don't think I'll ever get tired of saying it...

I LOVE YOU, JULIE. ❤️

Always remember that.
Always.

With all my love,
James ❤️

One last thing...

Will you keep this little piece
of my heart? ❤️
